Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

iPhone Stock (“the app”, https://iphone-stock-checker-hazel.vercel.app) is a free tool that notifies you when an iPhone is available for in-store pickup at an Apple Store near a US ZIP code. It is not affiliated with Apple Inc.

What we collect

  • Google account basics – when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We do not receive your password or access to any other Google data.
  • Your settings – the models, storage, colors, ZIP code, distance, ntfy topic, optional notification email, priority and language you choose.
  • Anonymous visit statistics – a random ID stored in your browser is used to count page views and unique visitors. We do not store IP addresses.

How we use it

  • To check Apple Store pickup availability for your ZIP code and send you notifications through ntfy.sh (and by email, if you add one).
  • To show the app owner aggregate visit statistics and the list of registered users.

We do not sell your data, show ads or share it with anyone except the services needed to run the app.

Services we use

  • Google Sign-In (authentication)
  • Vercel and Upstash (hosting and database)
  • GitHub Actions (scheduled stock checks)
  • ntfy.sh (push notifications)
  • Apple's public store availability data

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. Your language choice and the anonymous visitor ID are kept in your browser's local storage.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and settings, email lukavacklub1@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in your Google account permissions.
