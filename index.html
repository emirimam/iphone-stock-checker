How it works

This app watches Apple Store in-store pickup availability for iPhones near a US ZIP code. Every 15 minutes it checks Apple's stock, and when a phone you're looking for becomes available it sends a push notification to your phone. Then you order it online on apple.com with pickup at that store — that reserves the phone for you.

1. Sign in

Open Settings and click Sign in with Google. Each account has its own phone, location and notifications. Without signing in you can still browse stock for the default search.

2. Choose your phone

  1. In Settings → Which phone I'm looking for, select one or more models.
  2. Optionally select Storage and Color. Nothing selected means any.
  3. Enter the ZIP code where you'll pick it up and the max distance you're willing to drive.

3. Get notifications on your phone (ntfy)

  1. Install the free ntfy app from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open it, tap +, type a topic name only you know (e.g. john-iphone-7f3k9) and tap Subscribe. Anyone who knows the topic name can read it, so make it hard to guess.
  3. Enter the same topic in Settings → ntfy topic, click Save, then Send test notification. It should arrive within seconds.
  4. Optional: add an email to also get alerts by email, set Priority (Urgent rings longer) and Repeat reminder.

4. When you get an alert

  • Open the notification — it lists the model, the store and the distance.
  • Order on apple.com and choose pickup at that store. Stock changes quickly, so act fast.
  • Walking in without an order is possible but not guaranteed — walk-ins are served after online orders.

Privacy

From Google we store only your name, email and profile picture. Your settings are stored to run your alerts. Visits are counted anonymously (a random ID in your browser, no IP addresses).

Owner setup (app owner only)

  1. Go to Google Cloud Console → APIs & Services → Credentials (create a project if asked).
  2. OAuth consent screen: choose External, fill in the app name and your email, save. Then Publish app so anyone can sign in.
  3. Create credentials → OAuth client ID → type Web application.
  4. Under Authorized JavaScript origins add this page's address, e.g. https://iphone-stock-checker-hazel.vercel.app. No redirect URI is needed.
  5. Copy the Client ID (ends with .apps.googleusercontent.com) and paste it in the yellow setup box at the top of this page together with your PIN.
  6. Sign in with Google, open Settings and use Become owner with your PIN. The Admin tab with visitor stats appears.

FAQ

What does iPhone Stock do?

It checks Apple Store in-store pickup availability for the iPhone you want near your US ZIP code and sends you a push notification (or email) the moment it shows up, so you don't have to keep refreshing apple.com.

Which iPhones can I track?

iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone Air, iPhone 17 and iPhone 17e — every storage size and color Apple sells for pickup.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free, has no ads and you only need a Google account to save your alerts.

How often is stock checked?

Every few minutes, around the clock. You can also press “Check now” for an instant check.

How do I get notified?

Install the free ntfy app, subscribe to your own topic and enter it in Settings. You can also add an email address.

Does it work outside the US?

Not yet. It uses US ZIP codes and US Apple Stores.

Does it reserve or buy the phone?

No. When you get an alert, order on apple.com and choose in-store pickup at that store. That reserves the phone for you.

Is this an Apple app?

No. iPhone Stock is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Apple Inc. iPhone is a trademark of Apple Inc.

Stock is checked automatically every few minutes. “Check now” runs a check immediately. Not affiliated with Apple.
